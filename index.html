Three behaviours make this read as alive rather than as an animation: the iris chases the pointer on a spring so it lags like a real saccade landing, the lid blinks on an irregular timer, and the pupil dilates with pointer speed — a genuine autonomic response repurposed as a UI signal.

The fourth is the interesting one. Hold the pointer on the eye for two and a half seconds and it looks away, then blinks. Uncomfortable for three seconds, then charming — and it's the only interaction here that changes how people behave rather than how the page behaves.
